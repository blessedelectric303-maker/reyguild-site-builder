# ReyGuild Cookie Policy

> Sign-in cookies only. No advertising, no tracking.

---


**Last updated: 30 August 2026**

This policy explains the small files ReyGuild stores in your browser.

## 1. The short version

**ReyGuild uses cookies to keep you signed in, and for nothing else.** There is
no advertising, no tracking across other websites, no analytics profile, and no
third-party marketing tag anywhere in this app.

## 2. What is actually stored

**Authentication.** A session cookie proves you are signed in so that every
page you open does not ask for your password again. The command centre and the
T&M&P&L side each keep their own, which is why there are two.

**Security.** Short-lived values used to protect sign-in and password reset
from being replayed or forged.

**Preference.** Small values such as your chosen text size, so the app looks
the same next time you open it.

All of these are **strictly necessary or functional**. There are no advertising
or profiling cookies to consent to, which is why the app does not throw a
cookie banner at you every visit.

## 3. Local storage

The app may keep a small amount of data in your browser's local storage - for
example, checklist ticks so a half-finished checklist survives you closing the
page. It sits on your device, and clearing your browser data clears it.

## 4. Third parties

Infrastructure providers - hosting, database, mapping - may set their own
strictly necessary cookies while serving a request. We do not embed advertising
networks, social media pixels or third-party analytics.

## 5. Turning them off

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. **Blocking the
authentication cookie will stop you being able to sign in**, because there is
no other way for the app to remember you between pages.

## 6. Changes

If we ever add a cookie that is not strictly necessary, we will update this
page and ask for consent before setting it.
