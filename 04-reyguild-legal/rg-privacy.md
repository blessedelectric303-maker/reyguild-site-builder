# ReyGuild Privacy Policy

> What we collect, what we do with it, and what location tracking does and does not do.

---


**Last updated: 30 August 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains what ReyGuild - Blessed Electric LLC, a Colorado
limited liability company - collects, why, and what you can do about it.

**Two kinds of people are described here.** If your employer invited you into
ReyGuild, your employer decides what goes in and what comes out; we hold the
data on their instructions. If you are the company that subscribes, the
decisions are yours and the obligations to your staff are yours too.

## 1. What we collect

**You give us:**

- Name, email address, phone number, role
- Your password, stored only as a one-way hash - nobody here can read it
- Documents and files you upload: licence cards, tax forms, identification
  documents, voided checks, job photos, receipts
- Signatures: your typed name, the document and version you signed, and when

**Your work creates:**

- Clock in and clock out times, and the job each one belongs to
- **Location at the moment you clock in, clock out, or take another recorded
  action**, and the distance from the job address
- Jobs, hours, materials, purchases, absences, messages inside the app
- Customer records your company enters - names, addresses, phone numbers, job
  history

**Collected automatically:**

- IP address, browser and device type, and time, recorded with signatures and
  logins for security and for signature evidence
- Error and performance logs

## 2. Location, specifically

This is the part people care about most, so it is written plainly.

- We take a location reading **at the moment of an action** - a clock in, a
  clock out, an arrival. Not continuously
- We do **not** track a phone in the background. A web browser cannot do it
  when the page is closed, and we have not built a native app that could
- We store the coordinates, the time, and the distance from the job
- Your employer's owners and administrators can see it. Other technicians
  cannot
- Location is used to confirm attendance and distance, and for nothing else

## 3. Why we use it

- To run the Service and show you your work
- To keep time, pay and job records for your employer
- To prove who signed a document, and when
- To keep the Service secure and to investigate abuse
- To support you when you contact us
- To meet our own legal obligations

**We do not sell personal data, we do not share it for advertising, and we do
not use it to train artificial intelligence models.**

## 4. Who sees it

- **Your employer.** Owners and administrators see the records of the people in
  their company. This is the point of the product
- **Other users at your company**, to the limited extent the app shows them -
  a technician sees the office tier in messaging, not other technicians
- **Service providers** who run infrastructure for us under contract: hosting,
  database and storage, email delivery, address lookup. They may only process
  data to provide those services
- **Nobody else**, unless the law requires it, or a company is sold, in which
  case notice is given first

## 5. How long we keep it

- Account and work records: while the account is active, and for **30 days**
  after it closes so data can be exported
- Signatures and signed document versions: kept as long as the company's
  account exists, because their whole purpose is to be produced later
- Backups roll off on their own schedule, generally within 90 days
- A company can ask us to delete its data sooner. If you are an employee, ask
  your employer - they hold that decision, not us

## 6. Security

- Encrypted in transit and at rest
- Uploaded documents live in a **private** store and are only ever reachable
  through short-lived signed links
- Row-level database rules keep one company's data away from another's
- Passwords are hashed, never stored in readable form

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affecting personal data happens we
will notify affected companies without undue delay and as the law requires.

## 7. Your rights

Colorado residents have rights under the Colorado Privacy Act, and residents of
other states and countries may have similar ones. Subject to verification, you
may ask to:

- Know what personal data is held about you
- Get a copy of it in a portable form
- Correct it if it is wrong
- Delete it
- Opt out of sale or targeted advertising - **we do neither, so there is
  nothing here to opt out of**
- Appeal if we refuse a request

**How to ask.** If your employer invited you, ask your employer first - they
control the account and can usually do it faster. You may also write to us at
BlessedElectric303@gmail.com and we will respond within the time the law
allows, and pass the request on where the employer is the one who has to act.

## 8. Children

The Service is for workplaces and is not directed at anybody under 18. We do
not knowingly collect data from children.

## 9. Changes

We will post changes here with a new date, and give notice in the app for
material ones.
