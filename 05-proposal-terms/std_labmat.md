# Labor & Materials Included

## Customer sees this

All labor and all materials required to complete the work described in the line items above are included in the prices shown. There is no separate charge for labor, standard fasteners, connectors, wire, boxes, or consumables unless listed as its own line item.

Fixtures and specialty equipment are included only where specifically itemized above.

## Staff only

Goes on every proposal, first of the three. Stops the most common argument on an invoice - that the quoted price was labour only. Read it out loud when you present the price.
