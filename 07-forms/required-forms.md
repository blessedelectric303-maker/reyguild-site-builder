# Forms a new hire downloads or hands in

| Form | Required | Where to get it |
|---|---|---|
| Form I-9 - Employment Eligibility Verification | Yes | https://www.uscis.gov/i-9 |
| IRS Form W-4 - Employee's Withholding Certificate | Yes | https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-w-4 |
| Colorado Form DR 0004 - Employee Withholding Certificate | Optional | https://tax.colorado.gov/dr-0004-employee-resources |
| Direct deposit - voided check or bank letter | Yes | Upload only |
| Colorado electrical licence or apprentice registration card | Yes | https://dpo.colorado.gov/Electrical |
| Driver's licence | Yes | Upload only |
| IRS Form W-9 - for subcontractors only | Optional | https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-w-9 |

We link to the government page rather than hosting a copy. The day a form changes, our copy is the wrong one and somebody files it.
