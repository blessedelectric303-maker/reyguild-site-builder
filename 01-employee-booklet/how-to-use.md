> Start here. What this is and how the signing works.
>
> No signature - reference only

## This booklet is a set of agreements

This booklet is a set of binding agreements between you and Blessed Electric
LLC. It is separate from the Field Operations Handbook: the handbook tells you
how to do the work, this booklet tells you how to conduct yourself and what you
are agreeing to as a condition of employment.

## Read before you sign

Read every section before you sign anything. Each agreement has its own
signature block, and each one is signed separately. If you do not understand a
section, ask before you sign - not after.

**Do not sign a section you have not read.** Nothing here expires. You can open
any of it again at any time from Settings, Company Documents.

## Your copy

A signed copy is kept in your personnel file, and every page you sign stays in
this app with the date you signed it. You can come back and read what you
agreed to whenever you want.
