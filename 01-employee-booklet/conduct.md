# 1. Professional Conduct and Appearance

> How you look, how you speak, and how you behave in a customer's home.
>
> Signature required

---


## 1.1 You are the company

Every customer you meet forms their opinion of Blessed Electric from you alone.
They will never meet the owner, the office, or the rest of the crew. You are
the entire company as far as they are concerned.

**How you carry yourself in someone's home is not a personal matter. It is the
product we sell.**

## 1.2 Appearance standards

You will report to work and appear at every customer site:

- Clean and presentable - showered, groomed, no strong odors
- In company shirt - tucked, clean, not stained or torn
- Work pants in good condition - no rips, no sagging, no visible undergarments
- Work boots - clean, laced, in safe condition
- Facial hair trimmed and maintained
- Hair neat, under a hat or contained if long
- No offensive graphics, slogans, or language on any visible clothing or accessory

**Vehicle standards.** Company vehicles are part of your appearance. Cab clean,
no trash, no open food containers, no debris in the bed or rack, exterior
washed regularly. A dirty truck in a driveway costs us jobs we never even hear
about.

**On arrival at every home:** gloves, booties, and mask on, tools in hand,
before you knock. (Blessed Standard 1)

## 1.3 Conduct toward customers

You will at all times be:

- Polite - "please", "thank you", "yes ma'am", "no sir"
- Respectful of the home - you are a guest with tools, not a resident
- Patient - customers ask questions they have already asked. Answer them again, the same way, without sighing
- Calm under pressure - an upset customer is not an invitation to match their tone
- Honest - if you do not know, say you do not know and find out

You will not, under any circumstance:

- Argue with, mock, talk down to, or belittle a customer
- Criticize another contractor's work in an insulting way, or run down a competitor personally
- Discuss politics, religion, or other divisive topics in a customer's home
- Complain about the company, your pay, your schedule, your coworkers, or another customer in front of a customer
- Use profanity on a job site, in a customer's home, or anywhere you can be overheard
- Raise your voice at a customer for any reason

**The communication rule.** Never tell a customer "no" directly. Always respond
with "I'll let the boss know and he will follow up with you."

## 1.4 Conduct toward coworkers

- Speak to coworkers the way you would speak in front of a customer
- No horseplay, hazing, practical jokes, or roughhousing on a job site
- Disagreements are handled privately and calmly, or brought to a supervisor - never in front of a customer
- Help the person who is behind. We finish as a crew

## 1.5 Respect in the home

- Do not wander through areas of the home unrelated to your work
- Do not open drawers, cabinets, closets, refrigerators, or containers unless the work requires it
- Do not use customer bathrooms without asking permission
- Do not touch, handle, or move personal belongings beyond what the work requires - and put back anything you move
- Do not sit on customer furniture
- Do not bring food or drink into a customer's home beyond a sealed water bottle
- No smoking or vaping on customer property, in company vehicles, or anywhere in view of a customer
- Do not bring unauthorized passengers, family members, friends, or pets to a job site
- Never photograph or record inside a customer's home except for documented job purposes - and never with people in frame

## 1.6 Punctuality and time

- Arrive on time. Early is on time
- If you are going to be late, the customer is called before the arrival window passes, not after
- **Clock in and out honestly. Falsifying time records is theft and is grounds for immediate termination**
- Do not clock in for another employee, and do not allow another employee to clock in for you
- Breaks are taken away from customer view

## Acknowledgment

I have read and understand the Professional Conduct and Appearance standards
above. I agree to meet these standards at all times while representing Blessed
Electric LLC, and I understand that failing to do so may result in disciplinary
action up to and including termination.
