# 4. Non-Solicitation of Customers

> Company customers, confidential information, and the 12 months after you leave.
>
> Signature required

---


## 4.1 Why this exists

Blessed Electric LLC spends money and years building its customer relationships
- advertising, reputation, warranty work, and the trust built on every job. You
are introduced to those customers because of your employment here, not on your
own.

This section protects the company's customer relationships. It is not intended
to stop you from working as an electrician, from working for another employer,
or from earning a living in this trade.

## 4.2 What you agree to

During your employment with Blessed Electric LLC, you agree that you will NOT:

- Offer, advertise, or perform electrical work for a company customer outside of Blessed Electric LLC
- Solicit, encourage, or attempt to persuade any customer to take their work away from Blessed Electric LLC
- Refer a company customer to yourself, to a competitor, or to any other person or business for work Blessed Electric performs
- Give a customer a personal price, a personal quote, or a "cash deal" for any work
- Give a customer your personal contact information for the purpose of doing work directly
- **Accept payment directly from a customer for any work. All payments go through the office. No exceptions**
- Solicit or recruit Blessed Electric employees to leave and work elsewhere with you

**For a period of twelve (12) months after your employment ends**, you agree
that you will not directly solicit electrical work from any customer you
personally performed work for, or whose account information you had access to,
while employed by Blessed Electric LLC.

## 4.3 What this does not restrict

To be clear, this section does not prevent you from:

- Working for another electrical company after you leave
- Starting your own electrical business after you leave
- Performing work for a former Blessed Electric customer who contacts you on their own initiative, without you having solicited them
- Using the general skills, training, and experience you have as an electrician
- Discussing your wages or working conditions with coworkers or others, which is your protected right under law

## 4.4 Confidential company information

The following are confidential property of Blessed Electric LLC. You agree not
to copy, remove, share, sell, or use them outside your employment, and to
return or delete all copies when your employment ends:

- Customer names, addresses, phone numbers, emails, and job histories
- The Company Price List and all pricing charts, labor rates, and markups
- Estimates, proposals, contracts, and job photos
- Supplier accounts, vendor pricing, and account numbers
- Scheduling systems, logins, and app access
- This booklet and the Field Operations Handbook

**Colorado notice.** This does not restrict your right to discuss your own
wages, hours, or working conditions.

I agree to be bound by its terms during my employment and for twelve (12)
months after my employment ends.
