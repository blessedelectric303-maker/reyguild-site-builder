# 5. Unauthorized Side Work

> Working under the company name, and who is liable when you do not ask.
>
> Signature required

---


## 5.1 The rule

**You may not perform any electrical work under the Blessed Electric LLC name,
license, insurance, or reputation without the express written consent of the
owner.**

This includes work you are paid for, work you do as a favor, and work you do
for friends or family. If a customer, neighbor, or acquaintance believes
Blessed Electric is doing the job, then Blessed Electric is on the hook for it
- the license, the warranty, the liability, and the lawsuit.

## 5.2 What is prohibited

You will not, without written consent from the owner:

- Represent yourself as Blessed Electric LLC, or as acting on its behalf, on any work not scheduled through the company
- Use the Blessed Electric name, logo, license number, insurance, business cards, shirts, magnets, or vehicles in connection with any outside work
- Pull permits under the Blessed Electric license for any outside work
- Use company tools, equipment, materials, vehicles, or accounts for outside work
- Purchase material on a company account for any job not scheduled through the company
- Perform side work during scheduled company hours
- Perform side work for a Blessed Electric customer (see Non-Solicitation)

## 5.3 Liability for unauthorized work

**This is the part that matters most to you personally. Read it twice.**

If you perform electrical work outside of your employment with Blessed Electric
LLC without the company's written consent:

- You are acting entirely on your own behalf, as an independent individual - not as an employee, agent, or representative of Blessed Electric LLC
- Blessed Electric LLC is not responsible or liable in any way for that work, including any injury, death, property damage, fire, code violation, failed inspection, defect, or financial loss arising from it
- Blessed Electric LLC's insurance does not cover you for that work, and its license does not authorize it
- You accept full personal responsibility and liability for that work, including any warranty obligation, repair cost, legal claim, or damages
- You agree to indemnify and hold harmless Blessed Electric LLC - meaning you are responsible for any cost, claim, or legal expense the company incurs because you performed unauthorized work in its name

## 5.4 Requesting consent

If you want to take on outside work, ask first. Consent must be:

- Requested from the owner in advance
- Given in writing - a text or email from the owner counts, a verbal "yeah sure" does not
- Specific to the job. Consent for one job is not standing permission

Asking is free and nothing bad happens to you for asking. Not asking is what
ends employment.

## 5.5 Unlicensed work

You will not perform work that exceeds the scope of your license or
certification, or that requires a permit you have not obtained, whether on
company time or your own.
