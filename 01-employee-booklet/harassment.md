# 3. Harassment, Discrimination and Sexual Misconduct

> Zero tolerance, conduct alone in homes, and how to report.
>
> Signature required

---


## 3.1 Zero tolerance

Blessed Electric LLC has zero tolerance for harassment, discrimination, or
sexual misconduct of any kind - toward customers, toward coworkers, toward
vendors, or toward anyone you encounter in the course of your work.

**This is not a policy we bend for good techs, senior employees, or anyone
else.**

## 3.2 Discrimination is prohibited

You will not treat any customer, coworker, or applicant differently, or make
any remark about them, based on: race, color, national origin, ancestry,
religion, sex, gender, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, age,
disability, marital status, pregnancy, veteran status, or any other
characteristic protected by federal, Colorado, or local law.

## 3.3 Harassment is prohibited

Prohibited conduct includes, but is not limited to:

- Slurs, jokes, or "just kidding" comments about any protected characteristic
- Sexual comments, innuendo, propositions, or repeated unwanted attention
- Comments about a person's body, appearance, or clothing of a sexual nature
- Displaying, sending, or showing sexual or offensive images, videos, or messages
- Unwanted physical contact of any kind
- Intimidation, threats, bullying, stalking, or retaliation
- Any conduct that creates a hostile, offensive, or intimidating environment

## 3.4 Conduct in customer homes

You are frequently alone in homes with customers. Because of that, these rules
are absolute:

- **No romantic or sexual advances toward a customer, ever** - including flirting, comments, or asking a customer on a date
- Do not exchange personal contact information with a customer for personal reasons
- Do not contact a customer outside of work purposes, including social media
- The only permitted physical contact is the single brief shoulder or elbow reassurance described in the Field Operations Handbook - and only when the customer is clearly comfortable with it. When in doubt, do not touch the customer
- Never enter a bedroom or bathroom except when the work requires it, and inform the customer before you do
- If a customer behaves inappropriately toward you, leave and call your supervisor immediately. You will never be penalized for removing yourself from that situation

## 3.5 Reporting

If you experience or witness harassment, discrimination, or misconduct:

- Report it to the owner or Administrator immediately at 720-607-6440
- You may report in person, by phone, or in writing
- Reports will be taken seriously and investigated

**Retaliation against anyone who makes a good-faith report is itself a
terminable offense.** You will not lose your job, your hours, or your standing
for reporting something in good faith.
