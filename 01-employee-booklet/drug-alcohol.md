# 2. Drug and Alcohol-Free Workplace

> Impairment, prescriptions, testing, and getting help.
>
> Signature required

---


## 2.1 The policy

Blessed Electric LLC is a drug and alcohol-free workplace. Our employees work
with live electrical systems, ladders, power tools, and company vehicles, in
other people's homes. There is no version of impairment that is acceptable in
this trade.

**You will not report to work impaired. You will not become impaired at work.
There is no gray area here.**

## 2.2 What is prohibited

The following are prohibited and may result in immediate termination:

- Reporting to work under the influence of alcohol, marijuana, or any illegal drug
- Consuming alcohol, marijuana, or illegal drugs during your shift, during breaks, or during any paid time
- Consuming alcohol, marijuana, or illegal drugs in a company vehicle or on customer property at any time
- Possessing alcohol, marijuana, illegal drugs, or drug paraphernalia in a company vehicle, on a job site, or on customer property
- Selling, distributing, or offering drugs or alcohol to any coworker or customer
- Being impaired in any way that affects your judgment, coordination, speech, or safety

**Marijuana.** Although recreational marijuana is legal in Colorado, this
policy prohibits its use before or during work and prohibits reporting to work
impaired by it. Legality outside of work does not change the requirement to be
unimpaired at work.

## 2.3 Prescription medication

If you are taking a prescription or over-the-counter medication that may cause
drowsiness, dizziness, or impaired coordination or judgment, notify your
supervisor before your shift.

You do not have to disclose your medical condition or the name of the
medication - only that you may be affected and may need to be reassigned for
the day. Telling us is protected. Not telling us and getting hurt, or hurting
someone else, is not.

## 2.4 Reporting and testing

- If you believe a coworker is impaired on a job site, report it to a supervisor immediately. You are not "telling on" someone - you may be preventing an electrocution, a fall, or a crash
- The company may require drug or alcohol testing where permitted by law, including after a workplace accident or where there is reasonable suspicion of impairment
- Refusing a lawfully required test will be treated the same as a positive result

## 2.5 Getting help

If you have a substance problem and you come to us before it becomes a job
performance or safety issue, we will work with you on options where we
reasonably can. That door closes once you have shown up impaired, caused an
incident, or endangered someone. Coming forward voluntarily is always better
than being found out.
