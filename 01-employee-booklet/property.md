# 6. Company Property, Vehicles and Driving

> Tools, trucks, your licence, and never taking payment.
>
> Signature required

---


## 6.1 Tools, equipment and material

- Company tools and equipment are assigned to you and remain company property
- Report lost, stolen, damaged, or unsafe tools immediately - do not hide it and do not keep using it
- Company material is for company jobs only. Taking material for personal use is theft
- Return all tools, equipment, keys, uniforms, phones, and materials on your last day of employment

## 6.2 Company vehicles

- Company vehicles are for company business only unless you have written permission
- No unauthorized passengers - no family, friends, or non-employees
- Keep the vehicle clean inside and out
- Report every accident, no matter how minor, immediately - including parking lot scrapes and damage to customer property
- Report mechanical problems before they become breakdowns
- Fuel, maintenance, and mileage are logged as directed

## 6.3 Driving

- **You must hold a valid driver's license and maintain it.** Notify the office immediately if your license is suspended, revoked, or restricted
- Obey all traffic laws. Tickets and citations are your personal responsibility
- No handheld phone use while driving. Pull over
- No driving after consuming alcohol or any impairing substance, at any level
- You are representing the company every mile. Aggressive driving in a marked vehicle costs us customers

## 6.4 Customer payments

- **Never accept cash, check, or card payment directly from a customer.** All payment goes through the office
- If a customer offers you cash, decline politely: "Our office handles all payments - they'll be in touch with you."
- Never accept a tip in exchange for doing extra unbilled work
- Report any customer who pressures you on payment to your supervisor
