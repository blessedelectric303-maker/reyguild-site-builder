# 7. Safety

> The company safety policy and your duty to report.
>
> Signature required

---


## 7.1 Safety is not negotiable

No job, no schedule, and no customer is worth an injury. We would rather
reschedule a job than bury an employee.

## 7.2 Requirements

- Follow the National Electrical Code and all applicable state and local codes on every job
- **Verify de-energization before working on any circuit. Test it. Every time. Assume nothing**
- Use appropriate personal protective equipment
- Use ladders correctly and within their rating. Do not use customer furniture as a ladder
- Do not work on live systems where it can be avoided
- Never work alone on a task that requires two people
- Do not perform work you are not trained or qualified to perform - say so instead
- Follow lockout/tagout procedures where applicable

## 7.3 Reporting

- Report every injury immediately, no matter how small, and the same day it occurs
- Report every near-miss - a near-miss is a free lesson
- Report unsafe conditions on a job site before proceeding
- Report all property damage to the customer and to your supervisor before leaving the site

**You will never be disciplined for stopping work over a legitimate safety
concern. You may be disciplined for continuing anyway.**

## Acknowledgment

I have read and understand the Safety policy above. I agree to follow safe work
practices, to report injuries and unsafe conditions immediately, and to decline
work I am not qualified to perform.
