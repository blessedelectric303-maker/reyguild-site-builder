# 9. Disciplinary Process

> How problems are handled, and what ends employment on the spot.
>
> No signature - reference only

---


## 9.1 How we handle problems

Most problems get fixed with a conversation. Our normal progression is:

- **Verbal coaching** - we tell you what needs to change
- **Written warning** - documented, signed, placed in your file
- **Final written warning** - or suspension
- **Termination**

We do not have to follow these steps in order, and we may skip directly to
termination depending on the seriousness of the conduct.

## 9.2 Immediate termination

The following may result in termination without prior warning:

- Working impaired, or possession of drugs or alcohol on the job
- Harassment, discrimination, or sexual misconduct
- Theft of money, tools, material, or time
- Falsifying time records, estimates, invoices, or company documents
- Fighting or threats of violence
- Concealing damage or an injury
- Unauthorized work under the company name
- Soliciting company customers for personal work
- Serious safety violations that endanger yourself, a coworker, or a customer
- Three (3) unexcused absences within a 90-day period
- Job abandonment
