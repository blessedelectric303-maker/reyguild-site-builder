# 8. Social Media and Customer Privacy

> What stays in the house, and what never goes on a phone.
>
> Signature required

---


## 8.1 Customer privacy

Customers let us into their homes. What we see there stays there.

- Do not discuss a customer's home, family, finances, living conditions, or personal circumstances with anyone outside the company
- Do not share customer names, addresses, or contact information with anyone outside the company
- Do not photograph or record inside a home except for job documentation, and never with people, valuables, or personal items in frame
- Job photos belong to the company and are for company use

## 8.2 Social media

- **Do not post photos, videos, or descriptions of customer homes or job sites without the owner's written approval**
- Do not post anything that identifies a customer or their address
- Do not post about the company in a way that misrepresents it or damages its reputation
- Do not use the company name or logo on personal accounts in a way that suggests you speak for the company
- Nothing in this section restricts your legal right to discuss wages, hours, or working conditions
