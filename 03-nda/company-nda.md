# Company Nda

---

**Signed by everybody at [COMPANY NAME] who is given a login, except the
owner** - administrators, supervisors, technicians and apprentices all sign
this. It is separate from the ReyGuild Non-Disclosure Agreement, which covers
the software. This one covers the business.

## Why you are signing this

Your login gives you the inside of a business. Depending on your role that can
include every customer [COMPANY NAME] has, what it charges, what it pays, what
its margins are, who works there, and their signed personnel records.

None of that is yours, and none of it becomes yours by you having seen it.

**Administrators, read this twice.** An administrator can see every employee's
signed documents, uploaded identification, licence cards and the entire
customer database. The higher your access, the more of this falls on you
personally.

## What you agree to keep confidential

You agree never to copy, remove, photograph, screen shot, export, share,
publish, sell or use outside your work for [COMPANY NAME]:

- Customer names, addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, and job histories
- The price list, pricing charts, labor rates, material costs, markups and margins
- Estimates, proposals, contracts, invoices, and job photographs
- Supplier and vendor accounts, negotiated pricing, and account numbers
- Employee names, pay rates, hours, personnel files, and signed documents
- Logins, passwords, and access to any company system
- Any company procedure, checklist, script, standard operating procedure or handbook

## What this does not cover

Nothing here restricts you from:

- Discussing your own wages, hours, or working conditions with anybody, which
  is your protected right under federal and Colorado law
- Reporting a suspected violation of law to a government agency, or taking part
  in an agency investigation
- Using the general skills, training and trade knowledge you already had or
  developed in your trade
- Information that is genuinely public through no act of yours

You are not required to tell anybody before making a lawful report to a
government agency.

## Whistleblower notice

Under the federal Defend Trade Secrets Act, you cannot be held criminally or
civilly liable under any trade secret law for disclosing a trade secret in
confidence to a government official or an attorney, solely for the purpose of
reporting or investigating a suspected violation of law, or in a sealed court
filing.

## How long this lasts

This agreement takes effect the moment you are given access and continues for
the whole time you have it, and **for two (2) years after your access ends**,
for whatever reason it ends.

Anything that qualifies as a **trade secret** stays protected for as long as it
remains a trade secret under the law, which may be longer than two years.

## If you break this

You agree that a breach causes harm that money alone cannot fix, because
information once released cannot be recalled.

- An **injunction** may be sought to stop the breach, without a bond, in
  addition to any other remedy
- You may be held **personally and legally liable** for the resulting damages
- The prevailing party in any action to enforce this is entitled to recover
  reasonable attorney fees, court costs and related expenses
- A breach is grounds for immediate removal of your access

## General

Governed by the laws of the State of Colorado. If any provision is found
unenforceable, a court may narrow it to what is enforceable and the rest stays
in full force. This survives the end of your employment or access, and does not
by itself create an employment relationship or guarantee employment.

## When your employment ends

On your last day, or sooner if you are asked, you will:

- Return every company device, document, file and record in your possession
- Delete every copy held on a personal phone, computer, cloud account, email
  account or storage device
- Stop using any login, and not attempt to reach any system again
- Confirm in writing that you have done all of the above, if asked
